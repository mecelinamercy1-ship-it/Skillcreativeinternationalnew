Who We Are

Bridging technology, agriculture, energy and enterprise.

Skilled-Creatives International Limited exists to bridge the gap between technology, agriculture, renewable energy and entrepreneurship — through project-based learning and sustainable innovation. We don't just teach; we build workforces that create their own jobs.

From solar-powered classrooms to marketplace-ready graduates, every programme is designed to move a learner from theory to a tangible, income-generating outcome.

Organic Foods & Supplements

Fibre-rich, tradition-rooted nutrition — grown and processed locally.

Our organic line includes VegiFufu, Sweet Potato Mush Poundo, ShroomVita, Acha Flour and Oats Flour with Mushroom — formulated to support digestive and heart health using natural, fibre-rich ingredients like psyllium husk, cabbage and spinach.

These products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Drink adequate water, separate use from medications by at least two hours, and consult a healthcare provider before use if you have swallowing difficulties or a bowel obstruction.
