About Skilled-Creatives International

Skilled-Creatives International Limited connects people to the skills, products and opportunities that build real income. Our work runs across technology education, renewable energy, organic products, fashion and lifestyle, a marketplace, and support for innovation and entrepreneurship.

Our aim

To empower individuals and businesses through practical education, technology, innovation and sustainable opportunities.

Mission

Teach skills people can earn from, and give them the products and network to earn with.

Vision

A generation of skilled Nigerians building businesses that last, at home and beyond.
